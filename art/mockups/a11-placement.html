A staged frame of the real game (Ronin near the back, the CPU mid-court). Left: 1280×720. Right: 844×390, a phone with the touch controls where the game really puts them. The spectators are code-drawn placeholders in the palette's colours, only there to show place, size and what they cover. They are not the art. No credits spent.

Now (no crowd)

A Behind the back glass, along the bottom

  • Covers: the back of the floor (the strip from y 326 down, about the last 0.2 m of the court). A player at the back wall has their feet, and the ball's shadow and landing marker there, behind heads. It also hides the help line along the bottom ("Esc pause … Controllers …"), which would have to move.
  • Phone: the stick and HARD sit over its two ends.
  • The glass frame: its top rail (2.13 m) falls exactly on the front wall's floor line on screen, so it hardly shows. The posts fall on the court's outer edges.
  • The people: the biggest (heads 17–21 px), about 25, backs of heads only, so no faces. Reactions would be heads and arms. Seated heads behind real glass would sit below y 345 (15 px of hair), so it has to be pushed up to read at all.

B In the side margins

  • Covers on a computer: nothing. Left x 2–76, y 144–300 (under FIRST TO); right x 564–638, y 180–300 (under the logo); the key help stays clear.
  • Phone: it sits right under the stick (left) and SOFT / HARD (right). The buttons are see-through, so a moving crowd would show under your thumbs.
  • The people: heads 10 px, about 35, side-on facing the court. Narrow stacks, so it reads more like two boxes of fans than a stand.

C A gallery above the front wall (y 0–45)

  • Covers: nothing, on a computer or a phone. The panels start at y 16 but end at x 72, and the touch buttons are outside x 78–562.
  • The ball: a high shot that goes out over the front wall would cross the crowd for a few frames instead of the dark margin. It's black-outlined, so it should still show.
  • The people: small (heads 8 px, the size of a player at the front wall), about 90, facing the court and the camera. Faces barely read; reactions read as arms going up and colour moving.

D The gallery plus wings above the side walls (a mix; not in the plan's list)

  • What it adds: C, plus stands in the two empty dark triangles above the side walls (x 78–210 and 430–562, down to y 196 at the outer edge), so the court sits in an arena.
  • Covers: nothing. The wings stop at the side walls' top edge and at x 78. On a phone the stick's top only just meets the tip of the left wing.
  • Costs: busier, and the side walls' top edges lose their dark background, so the court's outline stands out less (darker or dimmer wings would help). About 150 people, still from a few pre-made looks.

For every option

  • The plan's spectator art is the same either way: a few concepts, 6–10 looks by recolouring, and idle, clap and cheer loops. The placement sets the size (A big, about 45×40 px; B, C, D small, about 16–24 px) and the view (A from behind; B side-on; C front; D front in the gallery, three-quarter in the wings).
  • The sounds (murmur, applause, cheer, groan) don't depend on the placement.
