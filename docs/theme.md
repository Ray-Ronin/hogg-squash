# Theme: Hogg

Hogg Squash is themed on **Hogg Middle School** (Houston ISD, https://hogg.houstonisd.org/) and the squash programme that plays on its courts,
**Mission Success / Mission Squash Academy** (https://missionsuccesshouston.org/), which runs Monday and Wednesday evenings at Hogg.
These are a general reference. Nothing has to match or be copied exactly.

## Hogg Middle School (from its website, 2026-10-18)
- **Mascot: the Razorbacks** (a wild boar).
- **Colours: red, black and white.** The site lists no exact colour codes, so we pick our own shades (spirit days are "Red Day" and "White Day"). **Chosen in A1, below.**
- **Logo:** a red and black shield reading "HOGG RAZORBACKS IB STEM" above a fierce boar's head, on white. Logo use is approved by the project owner.

## Mission Success (from its website)
- Founded 2011; an urban squash and academics after-school programme, "the only one of its kind in Texas".
- Brand colours: orange and teal (from its logo files).
- **No court photos** on the website. Not needed: the owner described the Hogg courts and shared a group photo taken on one.

## The Hogg courts (from the owner and a photo, 2026-10-18)
- **White walls**, with **red lines** (front-wall out-line, side-wall lines, floor short line, half-court line and service boxes).
- **Light wood floor** (pale maple-style boards).
- Bright, even lighting. (The photo itself isn't stored in the repo, since it shows students.)

## Ideas for using the theme (not decided)
- Hogg shield / "RAZORBACKS" on the front wall, on ad boards along the side walls, and on the title screen.
- **Decided:** the player is an adult in Hogg red and black. The CPU / P2 uses a palette swap to white and black (A1).
- A cartoon razorback as the title-screen mascot (and in the crowd, once there is one; no crowd in v1).
- Mission Success logo or colours on a wall board as a nod to the programme.
- **Decided (A4):** side-wall boards in Hogg red with white "HOGG" (left wall) and "RAZORBACKS" (right wall), each with a thin Mission Success orange-and-teal stripe along the bottom (the colours only, not their logo). No school shield anywhere until the school confirms (open question 6).
- **Decided (A5, the owner's call):** the school's Hogg Razorbacks shield is on the title screen, beside our own "HOGG SQUASH" lettering on a front-wall scene (`decisions.md`, Theme). It replaced the planned cartoon mascot, which is kept unused in the art source.
